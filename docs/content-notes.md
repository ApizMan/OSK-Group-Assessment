## Benefits
1. **Small groups**: Maximum 8 children per class, so every child gets help when they're stuck.
2. **No experience needed**: We start from zero. Curiosity is the only requirement.
3. **Projects they take home**: Every term ends with a game or animation your child can show you.
4. **Safe and supervised**: Two adults in every room, and we never ask for photos or personal accounts.

## How a trial class works
1. **Send an enquiry**: Tell us your child's age and which class looks fun.
2. **We confirm a time**: A real person replies within two working days.
3. **Your child tries a class**: Free, 60 minutes, and parents are welcome to stay.

## Parent FAQ
1. **Does my child need a laptop?** No. We provide laptops for every class.
2. **What if my child has never coded?** Perfect. Beginner classes assume no experience.
3. **How much do classes cost after the trial?** Terms are RM 360 for 8 weekly sessions. The trial is free.
4. **Is it safe?** Classes are supervised by two adults, and children only use our offline classroom accounts.
5. **What if my child doesn't enjoy it?** There's no obligation after the trial. If they join and change their mind in the first two weeks, we refund the rest of the term.
6. **Can siblings join together?** Yes, if they're in the same age group. Siblings get 10% off.
7. **How big are the classes?** No more than 8 children per class.
