Questions parents ask

Still unsure? Send an enquiry and a real person will reply.

Does my child need a laptop?

No laptop needed. We bring one for every child in every class.

What if my child has never coded?

Perfect. Beginner classes assume no experience.

How much do classes cost after the trial?

Terms are RM 360 for 8 weekly sessions. The trial is free.

Is it safe?

Classes are supervised by two adults, and children only use our offline classroom accounts.

What if my child doesn't enjoy it?

There's no obligation after the trial. If they join and change their mind in the first two weeks, we refund the rest of the term.

Can siblings join together?

Yes, if they're in the same age group. Siblings get 10% off.

How big are the classes?

No more than 8 children per class.
